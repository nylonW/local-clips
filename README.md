## Install

1. Download this repository as a ZIP and extract it.
2. Open [`chrome://extensions`](chrome://extensions) in Chrome.
3. Turn on **Developer mode**.
4. Click **Load unpacked** and select the extracted folder containing `manifest.json`.
5. Pin **Local Clips** to your toolbar.

## Save a clip

1. Open a live stream on Twitch or Kick.
2. Wait for the extension badge to turn green.
3. Click **Local Clips** and choose where to save the video.

Your clip is saved locally in its original quality as a `.ts` video file.

To change the clip length, right-click the extension icon, select **Options**, and choose between 15 seconds and 5 minutes. The default is 90 seconds.

## Good to know

- Clips stay on your computer and are never uploaded by the extension.
- No account, API key, or start-and-stop recording is needed.
- VLC, IINA, mpv, Chrome, and most video tools can play `.ts` files.
- Only recent available stream video can be saved; older moments may no longer be available.
- MP4 export is not currently supported.
